## Numark DJ2GO2 Pro Controller Compatibility - Learn with Dex 3 & RE Mapping Guide

[![Download Map for Numark DJ2GO2 DJ Controller](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/numarkdj2go2-coverimage.jpg?resize=706%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/numarkdj2go2-coverimage.jpg?fit=706%2C360&ssl=1 "Download Map for Numark DJ2GO2 DJ Controller")

**Download the Numark DJ2GO2 MAP file now (below) for plug-and-play DEX 3 and DEX 3 RE DJ software support!**

The latest addition to the list of 90+ [DEX 3 and DEX 3 RE supported DJ controllers](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) is the ultra-compact and highly-affordable [Numark DJ2GO2](https://www.numark.com/product/dj2go2). Just like it’s predecessor the Numark DJ2Go it’s small enough to fit in a large pocket (or Christmas stocking!) and ideal for a breakout house party or small event.

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![Numark DJ2GO2 DJ controller top](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/numark-dj2go2-1.jpg?fit=300%2C187&ssl=1 "Numark DJ2GO2 DJ controller top")](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/numark-dj2go2-1.jpg?fit=1030%2C644&ssl=1)

The **Numark DJ2GO2** Pocket DJ Controller has one major and surprising improvement over the previous model, especially for a controller that only costs $79 on most DJ equipment retailer websites, and that’s the built in Audio Interface. This makes headphone pre-fade listening (using [WASAPI](https://wiki.jriver.com/index.php/WASAPI) in Windows and [Core Audio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Core%5FAudio) on MAC) easy without the need for an additional USB audio interface.

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![Numark DJ2GO2 Mixer Section](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/numark-dj2go2-3.jpg?fit=300%2C187&ssl=1 "Numark DJ2GO2 Mixer Section")](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/numark-dj2go2-3.jpg?fit=1030%2C644&ssl=1)

The **Numark DJ2GO2** is sized to fit directly in front of most laptops (about the same width of a 15″ laptop keyboard) and provides full 2-deck control over [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) or [DEX 3 RE](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/). It takes only a few seconds to setup the **DJ2GO2** as it doesn’t require driver installation or additional hardware. Simply connect the USB cable between your computer and the controller, connect a pair of headphones and you’re ready to play.

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![Numark DJ2GO2 Connections](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/numark-dj2go2-4.jpg?fit=300%2C187&ssl=1 "Numark DJ2GO2 Connections")](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/numark-dj2go2-4.jpg?fit=1030%2C644&ssl=1)

The **Numark DJ2GO2** layout features tactile hands-on controls which are all supported in [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) and [DEX 3 RE](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) mixing software. The **DJ2Go2** include two platters, a crossfader (which lends itself well to both audio and in the case of [DEX 3 (pro edition)](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), blending between music videos), pitch and level controls, as well as Deck A, Deck B, Master and Headphone gain controls. In addition, you can load and assign tracks to either of the two included deck control sections without ever touching your computer, which wasn’t available on first version of the **DJ2Go**.

**DJ2GO2** fits on top of your laptop and is well suited for small gigs, pop-ups, track preparation, and also serves as a great back up for those “just in case” moments.

Bottom line, the **Numark DJGO2** is a quality upgrade from the previous model and it now pairs perfectly with [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) or [DEX 3 RE](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) for those on-the-go DJ requirements!
